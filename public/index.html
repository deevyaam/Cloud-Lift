      How it works

      1. Pick a direction with the two large tiles.
      2. Connect Microsoft and Google once. Your sign-ins are remembered and renewed automatically.
      3. Choose what to copy and where it goes, review, and press Start.

      OneDrive to Google

      • Automatic sends photos and videos to Google Photos and everything else to Google Drive.
      • Turn on Keep Google Takeout dates and locations when copying a Takeout export that is stored in OneDrive.

      Google to OneDrive

      • Google Photos: Google only lets apps read the items you pick, so press Choose in Google Photos, select items and press Done. Google removes the location from these copies.
      • Google Drive: any folder. Docs, Sheets and Slides are saved as Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.
      • Takeout export: best for a whole library. Extract the .zip files into one folder and turn on the dates and locations option.

      Good to know

      • Your originals are never changed or deleted. Existing files at the destination are never overwritten.
      • Pause and resume at any time. Transfers continue after the app restarts, and pause if a sign-in expires.
