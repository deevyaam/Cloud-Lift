        How CloudLift works

        1. Connect accounts. Sign in to Microsoft (where your files are) and Google (where they go).
        2. Choose a OneDrive folder. Open folders by clicking them, then press Select this folder. Everything inside, including subfolders, is copied.
        3. Choose a destination and start. Auto sends photos and videos to Google Photos and everything else to Google Drive.

        Google → OneDrive

        Switch the direction at the top of the page. In step 2 pick the OneDrive folder to copy into, then in step 3 choose a source:

        • Google Photos: Google only lets apps read the photos you pick, so press Choose in Google Photos, select items and press Done. Repeat for more.
        • Google Drive: pick any Drive folder. Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are saved as Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.
        • Google Takeout: best for a whole Photos library. Export from takeout.google.com, extract all the .zip files into one folder, and paste its path. Turn on metadata merge to keep dates and locations.

        Good to know

        • Your files stay in OneDrive. CloudLift only copies them.
        • You can pause and resume at any time. Transfers also resume after the server restarts.
        • If a sign-in expires, the transfer pauses and continues once you reconnect.
        • The filter box at the top filters the folders you are looking at in step 2.
